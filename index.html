Assistive Technology Trainer Knowledge Tests

Choose a product below to take a short knowledge test. Each test has 15 multiple-choice questions covering that product's core features, keyboard commands, and appropriate use. Results and explanations are shown immediately after you submit. Questions and answer order are shuffled each time. A study mode (review without grading) is available for every test, and your past attempts are saved on this device — see your test history.
